Your phone is the football

Your phone is the ball

The bottom of the phone is the nose of the ball.
Ready: hold it low in front of you and still, the bottom pointing to your throwing side, palm toward you.
Hike: bring it up quickly to where you throw from.
Throw it at a receiver — keep hold of the phone! Your normal throw is a bullet; throw it easier (or let go high) for a lob.
